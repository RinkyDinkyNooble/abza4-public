# Changelog

## [4.1.1] CLIENT and SERVER

Carries on in a 4.1.0 world. A server needs the 4.1.1 server pack.

### New since 4.1.0
- A new questbook. Each tier is a map of three lines, Survive, Tech and Gear, from where you arrive to the tier's vaccine. Steps tick as you play, in any order.
- Each tier lists its own ores; the Plains, the Snow and the Badlands show a chart of their heights.
- Backpacks, armour sets, grappling hooks, vehicles, rockets and fuel each have steps in their tier.
- Records: containers looted and horde nights survived.
- Client Sort: sort, fill and move items with buttons on chests and loot containers.
- Starter kits give stone tools instead of wooden ones. The Scavenger's kit no longer has a backpack.
- Plazas, hydro gardens and playgrounds are rarer.
- Hired guards sit higher in vehicle seats.
- The Tier 2 Material Pack takes an emerald in place of one lead ingot.
- Includes 4.1.0-hotfix's JEI fix for servers.

## [4.1.0-hotfix] CLIENT

- On a server, JEI lists every item and its recipes again (only fluids showed). Singleplayer wasn't affected.
- Joins any 4.1.0 server: the server pack is unchanged.

## [4.1.0] CLIENT and SERVER

The Plains are complete. **A new world is recommended:** the new buildings only appear where the world hasn't been generated yet.

### New since 4.0.1
- 24 new buildings in the Plains, 32 in all: a café, a bank, offices, a police station, a clinic, a marina, a museum and more. 28 of them also come in a darker look, and buildings now stand turned every way, so streets repeat less.
- The museum is a boss building: bosses on the way, and the Ancient Zombie in its sealed lab.
- Keypad and keycard doors lock the best rooms; the code or card is usually in the same building.
- People in the city: the Survivors' holdout with traders and guards, a lone trader on a vacant lot, and raiders taking a toll on others.
- Some marinas have a speedboat, and some police checkpoints a pickup with a heavy machine gun.
- Highways cross the land in long straight lines, about 1,000 blocks apart.
- You start in a cherry grove, away from the cities.
- ABZA's own music: a new menu theme, three tracks on the Plains and one for everywhere else.
- ABZA Shader 1.1.0.
- New title screen pictures and loading screen; fewer, clearer loading tips.
- Chat can't be reported to Mojang. No Chat Reports is gone: a script in the pack does its job.
- The server pack holds only the mods a server runs, so hosts that install from its manifest work. Its start scripts skip the Java check: set your host to Java 17.

### Still a stand-in until the later cities
- Mini-bosses, bosses and final bosses come to players in their region; they drop finds from the region's loot.
- Outside the Plains, creatures drop finds from what a city's containers hold.
- The first time someone reaches a region, its traders make camp nearby, with guards for hire from the Snow on.
- The Alien Navigation Core drops from the Badlands' final boss and, sometimes, its bosses.

## [4.0.1] CLIENT and SERVER

The first alpha. **Start a new world.** Version numbers are plain from now on: the major number is the tier (4 for the Plains).

### New since 4.0.0-prealpha
- The Plains' city is ABZA's own: detached houses, townhouses, bungalows, a corner shop, an apartment tower, a pharmacy, a car park and a plaza, with loot, safes and notes. Creatures roam the streets, and open lots are grass.
- Lost Cities' default cities are gone: the Snow and the Badlands have no cities until theirs are built.
- Prospero, the yacht with the traders, on the ocean nearest spawn.
- ABZA's title screen, menus and loading screen.
- Tips on the loading screen, and credits (`/credits`).
- Every player is on one team, with friendly fire off.
- Vanilla's music is off in game; the menus play their own track.
- The Plains' common creatures drop no finds (the city's loot replaces them).

### Stand-in until the buildings
- Mini-bosses, bosses and final bosses come to players in their region; they drop finds from the region's loot.
- Outside the Plains, creatures drop finds from what a city's containers hold.
- The first time someone reaches a region past the Plains, its traders and guards for hire make camp nearby.
- The Alien Navigation Core drops from the Badlands' final boss and bosses.

## [4.0.0-prealpha] CLIENT

### New
- Eight tiers across Earth, the Moon and four new worlds, each with its own creatures, final boss, ores, climate and affliction.
- Vaccines, medicine by tier, blood packs, and a death penalty on max health.
- Max health from final bosses and the Bestiary.
- Guns rebalanced by tier, each tier with its own rounds; ammo boxes; repairs.
- A Mekanism tech ladder to rockets, with ABZA's own materials and planet metals.
- Horde nights every fourth night, climbing with each one faced.
- Air drops called by flare guns.
- Temperature by region, with armour protection levels.
- A questbook: the essentials, progression per tier and a Bestiary.
- Starter kits: Soldier, Scout or Scavenger.

### Stand-in until the buildings
- Lost Cities' default cities, their chests filled with ABZA's loot by region.
- Mini-bosses, bosses and final bosses come to players in their region; they drop finds from the region's loot.
- The Alien Navigation Core drops from the Badlands' final boss and bosses.
