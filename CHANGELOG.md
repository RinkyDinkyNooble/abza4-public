# Changelog

## [4.0.0-prealpha] CLIENT - unreleased

A rebuild of the pack from 3.0.1. **Start a new world.**

### New
- Eight tiers across Earth, the Moon and four new worlds, each with its own creatures, final boss, ores, climate and affliction.
- Vaccines, medicine by tier, blood packs, and a death penalty on max health.
- Max health from final bosses and the Bestiary.
- Guns rebalanced by tier, each tier with its own rounds; ammo boxes; repairs.
- A Mekanism tech ladder to rockets, with ABZA's own materials and planet metals.
- Horde nights every fourth night, climbing with each one faced.
- Air drops called by flare guns.
- Temperature by region, with armour protection levels.
- A questbook: the essentials, progression per tier and a Bestiary.
- Starter kits: Soldier, Scout or Scavenger.

### Stand-in until the buildings
- Lost Cities' default cities, their chests filled with ABZA's loot by region.
- Mini-bosses, bosses and final bosses come to players in their region; they drop finds from the region's loot.
- The Alien Navigation Core drops from the Badlands' final boss and bosses.
